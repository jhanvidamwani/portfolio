More experiments and research

  • SpyHacks, team of three

    GradNest

    A soft landing for new grad students. GradNest is an AI-powered sublease board and survival guide just for Stevens grad students.

    Read the case study
  • AI product

    EquiTea

    Equity research in minutes. EquiTea retrieves SEC filings on demand and turns them into Buy, Hold or Sell recommendations, with the passages behind each call.

    Read the case study
  • Research assistant, Stevens

    Crypto Value-at-Risk research

    Reproduced Prof. Lakshminarayan's group's VaR analysis on 10 cryptocurrencies, matching it exactly. Now extending it from 10 to 20 assets.

  • Financial ML

    FinBERT vs. fine-tuned MLP

    Compared pretrained FinBERT with a fine-tuned MLP head on WSJ articles against S&P 500 daily returns, 2017 to 2020. FinBERT reached 53.1% directional accuracy, the fine-tuned model 45.9%, and neither explained returns (near-zero R²). An honest result.

  • Paper replication, with Yashika Varshney

    CNN stock price prediction

    Re-implemented Jiang, Kelly and Xiu (2023), "Re-Imag(in)ing Price Trends": CNNs trained on ~470K chart images to predict 5-day moves, three architectures, time split over 2010 to 2019. Test AUC was 0.457, which we read as a market regime change.

  • Independent research paper

    Fair by Design

    My own research paper on algorithmic fairness in dynamic pricing: when personalised prices stop being smart and start being unfair.

  • DCC-GARCH, Monte Carlo

    Cross-asset contagion risk model

    Modelled how stress spreads between asset classes using DCC-GARCH correlations and Monte Carlo simulation.

  • Top 3 on Kaggle

    Credit default model

    A loan default prediction model that finished top 3 on Kaggle (RMSE 0.0595).

How I thinkdata, insight, then a decision

How I thinkp. 7

One habit, three steps.

  1. Data. Thousands of rows, mostly noise. I start by getting close to it.
  2. Insight. Find the pattern that actually moves, and make it easy to see.
  3. Strategy. Turn the pattern into a decision someone can act on.
Worked example · StockSwipe

Thirty headlines about one stock in a week. FinBERT sorts them: 15 positive, 9 neutral, 6 negative. A beginner doesn’t need the thirty headlines. They need one card that says mostly positive this week, and a choice: swipe right or left.

Illustrative counts.

“The decision is the product.”

Editor’s letterJhanvi · Issue 01p. 44

the person behind it.

Hi, I'm Jhanvi. I live in New York and I'm doing my master's in Financial Technology Analytics at Stevens (4.0 so far, finishing May 2027).

I got into brand work sideways. At AlphaSense I spent my days reading how thousands of companies describe themselves, and I ended up the top analyst on a team of eight. In college I ran the creative side of our media club with a team of 50. Somewhere between the two, I noticed what I actually care about: why one brand feels trustworthy and another doesn't.

So that's what I work on now. I start with the data, use it to work out what a brand should say and look like, and build the tool myself when one is missing.
