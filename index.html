ProjectVaultAI project-management platform

My role Product designer and builder

On busy teams, the important stuff lives in scattered threads, docs and meetings. People lose context, repeat work and miss what changed.

ProjectVault is an AI project-management platform that keeps one shared memory of a team's work. It pulls relevant insights out of everyday workflows automatically, so nobody has to dig for them.

“I spot a daily workflow pain and design the product around it.”
  1. 1Capture Work from across the team lands in one place instead of five tabs.
  2. 2Remember Everything is stored as vectors in pgvector, so context is never lost.
  3. 3Surface Claude pulls out the insight that matters right now and puts it in front of you.

EquiTeaAI product

My role Product designer and builder

Diligence research is slow and manual. Analysts dig through hundreds of pages of 10-Ks and 10-Qs before they can form a view.

EquiTea retrieves SEC filings on demand and turns them into Buy, Hold or Sell recommendations, with the passages behind each call. I designed the features and the flow around how analysts actually work.

“I treat trust as a product feature: every answer shows its source.”
  1. 1Collect Pull a company's recent filings straight from SEC EDGAR.
  2. 2Retrieve Find the passages that matter for revenue, risk, guidance and balance-sheet health.
  3. 3Recommend Return a Buy, Hold or Sell view with its evidence attached.

Python · Claude API · RAG · SEC EDGAR

SentinelChubb challenge, Stevens Business + AI Hackathon, Sep 2026

My role Product lead and builder. Built with Amrit Chhabda

Insurers need to see catastrophe exposure before it becomes a loss. Underwriters covering energy and technology risks usually hear about trouble from scattered sources, after exposure has already moved.

Sentinel is a two-agent dashboard that helps underwriters watch energy and tech exposures and act earlier. One agent watches for early signals; the other turns them into what an underwriter needs to decide. Chubb's early-career team noticed the work and invited me to connect.

“I design AI around one user and the decision they have to make.”
  1. 1Watch The first agent scans incoming signals for events that could become a loss in energy or tech portfolios.
  2. 2Interpret The second agent frames each signal in underwriting terms: what is exposed, how severe it looks and how sure the read is.
  3. 3Act Everything lands on one dashboard, so an underwriter can see the risk picture and move first.

Multi-agent AI · Risk dashboard · GitHub Pages

StockSwipeTeam project, Stevens Institute of Technology

My role Built almost all of it: the product idea, the design and the app. With Yashika Varshney, who wrote the final report. The repo lives under her GitHub.

Most retail investors tune out when market data gets too dense. Screeners and tables are built for professionals, so beginners never get past the first page.

StockSwipe is a Tinder-style stock discovery app. Instead of a screener, every stock is a card you swipe right or left on. I built nearly all of it, from the idea and the design to the app itself, to make investing visual and low-effort and let the app learn what each person cares about.

“I design for the person who usually gets left out of financial products.”
  1. 1Read the news FinBERT scores the sentiment of recent news for each stock.
  2. 2Swipe Each stock becomes one simple card. Right if you like it, left if you don't.
  3. 3Learn Reinforcement learning personalizes the feed to what you swipe on.

FinBERT · Reinforcement learning · News sentiment · Vercel

More experiments and research

  • Research assistant, Stevens

    Crypto Value-at-Risk research

    Reproduced Prof. Lakshminarayan's group's VaR analysis on 10 cryptocurrencies, matching it exactly. Now extending it from 10 to 20 assets.

  • Financial ML

    FinBERT vs. fine-tuned MLP

    Compared pretrained FinBERT with a fine-tuned MLP head on WSJ articles against S&P 500 daily returns, 2017 to 2020. FinBERT reached 53.1% directional accuracy, the fine-tuned model 45.9%, and neither explained returns (near-zero R²). An honest result.

  • Paper replication, with Yashika Varshney

    CNN stock price prediction

    Re-implemented Jiang, Kelly and Xiu (2023), "Re-Imag(in)ing Price Trends": CNNs trained on ~470K chart images to predict 5-day moves, three architectures, time split over 2010 to 2019. Test AUC was 0.457, which we read as a market regime change.

  • Independent research paper

    Fair by Design

    My own research paper on algorithmic fairness in dynamic pricing: when personalised prices stop being smart and start being unfair.

  • DCC-GARCH, Monte Carlo

    Cross-asset contagion risk model

    Modelled how stress spreads between asset classes using DCC-GARCH correlations and Monte Carlo simulation.

  • Top 3 on Kaggle

    Credit default model

    A loan default prediction model that finished top 3 on Kaggle (RMSE 0.0595).

Editor’s letterJhanvi · Issue 01p. 44

the person behind it.

Hi, I'm Jhanvi. I live in New York and I'm doing my master's in Financial Technology Analytics at Stevens (4.0 so far, finishing May 2027).

I got into brand work sideways. At AlphaSense I spent my days reading how thousands of companies describe themselves, and I ended up the top analyst on a team of eight. In college I ran the creative side of our media club with a team of 50. Somewhere between the two, I noticed what I actually care about: why one brand feels trustworthy and another doesn't.

So that's what I work on now. I start with the data, use it to work out what a brand should say and look like, and build the tool myself when one is missing.
