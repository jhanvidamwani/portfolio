AI product

EquiTea

Equity research in minutes.

Role

Product designer and builder

Built with

Python, Claude API, RAG, SEC EDGAR

GitHub

The problem

Diligence research is slow and manual. Analysts dig through hundreds of pages of 10-Ks and 10-Qs before they can form a view.

What I built

EquiTea retrieves SEC filings on demand and turns them into Buy, Hold or Sell recommendations, with the passages behind each call. I designed the features and the flow around how analysts actually work.

How it works

  1. 1

    Collect

    Pull a company's recent filings straight from SEC EDGAR.

  2. 2

    Retrieve

    Find the passages that matter for revenue, risk, guidance and balance-sheet health.

  3. 3

    Recommend

    Return a Buy, Hold or Sell view with its evidence attached.

What it shows about how I work

  • I treat trust as a product feature: every answer shows its source.
  • I know how analysts read filings, from years of doing it.
  • I can make a complex AI system feel simple.
