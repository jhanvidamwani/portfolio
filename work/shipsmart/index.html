ShipSmart

No more "where is my order?"

Role

Product framing and pitch

Built with

Product strategy, B2B SaaS, 12-slide pitch

The problem

Last-mile delivery is a black box. Restaurants and shops waiting on perishable goods can't see what's happening, so they flood logistics companies with "where is my order" tickets.

What I built

ShipSmart is a B2B transparency layer for last-mile delivery. I framed the whole product around Rachel, a Jersey City restaurant owner waiting on perishable goods, and told the story in a 12-slide deck. It placed in the top 4.

How it works

  1. 1

    Context

    ETAs that explain why, not just when, so partners can plan around delays.

  2. 2

    Safety and cost

    Driver safety scorecards and clear cost breakdowns for logistics teams.

  3. 3

    Heads-up

    Proactive delay alerts that reach the customer before they have to ask.

What it shows about how I work

  • I start with one real person and design outwards from her problem.
  • I can turn an operations product into a story people remember.
  • I think about both sides of a marketplace at once.
