The problem

Most retail investors tune out when market data gets too dense. Screeners and tables are built for professionals, so beginners never get past the first page.

What I built

StockSwipe is a Tinder-style stock discovery app. Instead of a screener, every stock is a card you swipe right or left on. I built nearly all of it, from the idea and the design to the app itself, to make investing visual and low-effort and let the app learn what each person cares about.

How it works

  1. 1

    Read the news

    FinBERT scores the sentiment of recent news for each stock.

  2. 2

    Swipe

    Each stock becomes one simple card. Right if you like it, left if you don't.

  3. 3

    Learn

    Reinforcement learning personalizes the feed to what you swipe on.

What it shows about how I work

  • I design for the person who usually gets left out of financial products.
  • I borrow familiar behaviour from consumer apps to make finance feel easy.
  • I can turn a model's output into an interaction people actually enjoy.
