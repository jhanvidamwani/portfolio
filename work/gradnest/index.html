GradNest

A soft landing for new grad students.

Role

Led the AI features and the Streamlit frontend

Built with

Gemini, Groq + Llama, Streamlit, Stevens catalog data

The problem

Moving to Jersey City for grad school, I learned how stressful it is to find housing from abroad: risky leases, scattered listings and no one to ask.

What I built

GradNest is an AI-powered sublease board and survival guide just for Stevens grad students. I led the AI features and built the Streamlit frontend. The idea came straight from my own move.

How it works

  1. 1

    Find a place

    A Stevens-only sublease board, plus roommate matching.

  2. 2

    Check the lease

    An AI red-flag checker (Gemini) reads the lease and flags what to worry about.

  3. 3

    Ask anything

    A housing chat agent (Groq with Llama) and course tips from real Stevens catalog data.

What it shows about how I work

  • I build from problems I've lived through myself.
  • I use AI where it lowers real risk for people, like a bad lease.
  • I can lead the AI and the front end in a 24-hour team build.
